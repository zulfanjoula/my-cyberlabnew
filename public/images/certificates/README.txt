HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL CERTIFICATE IMAGES:
=========================================

1. Copy your real certificate image files into this directory:
   public/images/certificates/

2. Name them appropriately, for example:
   - certificate-01.jpg
   - certificate-02.jpg
   - certificate-03.jpg
   (or .png / .webp)

3. Update the matching entry in:
   src/data/certificates.ts

4. You can easily adjust:
   - title
   - issuer
   - date
   - category
   - shortDescription
   - fullDescription (your detailed reflection of what you learned)
   - credentialUrl (link to verify credential if available)
